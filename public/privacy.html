Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

StudyPack (studypack-generator.vercel.app) turns lecture notes into study guides, flashcards and quizzes. It is run by an independent developer in Nigeria. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can have it deleted. Questions: amulukyle1234@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your lecture files are read on your own device. The file itself is never uploaded.
  • When you ask StudyPack to write a pack, answer a question or read scanned pages, the text (and any pictures it needs) is sent to an AI model to do that job, then not kept.
  • If you sign in, we keep your email, name and saved packs so they follow you to every device.
  • Payments are handled by Paystack or Gumroad. We never see your card or bank details.
  • No ads, no tracking cookies, and we never sell your data. We count page visits without cookies.
  • Email us and we'll delete your account and data.

1. What we collect and why

Your lecture notes

PDFs, PowerPoints and photos you add are opened and read in your browser. The file stays on your device (it's kept in your browser's storage so you can look back at the lecture behind each card and question). It is not uploaded to us.

When you choose to generate a study pack, ask a question about a pack, or have scanned pages read, the text of your notes (and, where the feature needs it, pictures of diagram pages or scanned pages) is sent to our server and on to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, which we use through Amazon Bedrock. It is used only to write your result. A finished pack waits on our server for up to an hour so your phone can collect it, then it's deleted. We don't keep your notes.

If you paste a YouTube link, we fetch that video's captions through Supadata to turn them into notes.

Your account

You can use parts of StudyPack without an account. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. If you sign in with an email code, we only have your email address; the code is sent through our email provider (Brevo or Resend). We use these to keep you signed in, to show your account, and to link your saved packs and plan to you.

Your saved packs

Signed out, your library stays in your browser on that device. Signed in, your study packs (the generated guides, flashcards and quizzes, not your original files) are also saved in our database so they appear on all your devices.

If you choose to share a pack to the Pack library, that pack becomes visible to every StudyPack user. It is shown without your name or email. You, or we, can remove it at any time.

Payments

Payments in naira are processed by Paystack, and payments in dollars by Gumroad. They collect and protect your card, bank or mobile money details under their own privacy policies; we never see or store them. From them we receive and keep your email, a customer code, the payment reference and amount, which plan you bought and when it runs until, and whether auto-renew is on.

Usage, invites and abuse prevention

  • Usage: how much AI work each account has used, so plan limits can be applied. These counters reset each period.
  • Invites and share links: which link first brought you to StudyPack (for example a friend's invite link), and, for your own invite link, how many friends joined and paid, so rewards can be given.
  • Sign-in protection: to stop sign-in codes being abused, we count requests per email address and per IP address for an hour. The IP address is stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value.
  • Server logs: our hosting keeps short-lived technical logs (errors, timings, your account ID and costs of AI requests) to keep the service working.
  • Reviews: if you rate StudyPack, we keep your rating, comment, first name, email and plan so we can improve the app and reply to you. Your first name and comment are shown publicly only if you tick the box allowing it; your email never is. Ask us and we'll delete your review.
  • Visit counts: we count page visits with Vercel Web Analytics to see which screens are used and which links bring people (for example X or WhatsApp). It uses no cookies, doesn't record your name, email or what's in your notes, and can't follow you across other websites.

On your device

StudyPack stores your library, flashcard and quiz progress, study streak, lecture files and settings in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB) so it works quickly and offline. Clearing your browser's site data removes them. We use two cookies, both needed to sign you in: a session cookie (30 days) and, during email sign-in, a code cookie (10 minutes). We use no advertising or analytics cookies. Google's sign-in button is loaded from Google, which may set its own cookies under Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. We share it only with the services that run StudyPack, for the jobs above:

  • Amazon Web Services (Amazon Bedrock): runs the Claude AI model on your notes. Amazon Bedrock doesn't use your content to train AI models and doesn't share it with the model's maker.
  • Vercel: hosts the website and server, and counts page visits (Vercel Web Analytics, without cookies).
  • Upstash: our database (accounts, saved packs, plans, usage counters).
  • Google: sign-in with Google.
  • Brevo or Resend: sends sign-in code emails.
  • Paystack and Gumroad: payments.
  • Supadata: fetches YouTube captions, when you use a YouTube link.

StudyPack's Discord community is run on Discord, a separate service. If you join it, what you post there is covered by Discord's Privacy Policy, and other members can see it.

These providers may process data outside Nigeria (for example in the United States or Europe). We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect StudyPack and its users from fraud or abuse.

3. How long we keep it

  • Notes sent for a pack, answer or scan: only while the job runs; the finished pack is collected within an hour, then deleted.
  • Account details and saved packs: until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.
  • Shared packs: until you or we remove them.
  • Payment records: for as long as tax and accounting laws require, even after an account is deleted.
  • Sign-in protection counters: about an hour. Usage counters: until the end of the plan period, plus a day.

4. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (and similar laws where you live), you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to get a copy, to object to how we use it, or to have it deleted. Email amulukyle1234@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll reply within 30 days. You can delete individual packs yourself at any time from your library. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

5. Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in uses a signed, HttpOnly cookie that scripts can't read, and secret keys stay on our server. No system is perfectly secure, but we only keep what StudyPack needs.

6. Children

StudyPack is for students aged 13 and over. If you're under 18, please use it with a parent's or guardian's permission, and ask them before buying a plan. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, email us and we'll delete it.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top, and for important changes we'll tell signed-in users in the app or by email.
